### About Me

Hello, my name is Fernando, I work as a software developer and on my free time I like to tinker with my homelab, that I mostly use it to host songs and personal files, I also like to  play games, watching anime and reading manga/LN on my free time.

I am creating this site to share some things that I find interesting, I'll be talking about homelab, about the apps I am hosting, recommendations, and so one. I know nothing I will writing here will be original nor will it be that informative compare to other places, but I will try my best. Maybe I will even talk about my other hobies, but only if I find something interesting.

You may be thinking "Why are you doing this? Isn't there better resources about the things you will talk about? are you a expert of some kind?". 

For the first question the answer is that I have grow tired of my incompetence, I am always thinking "I should do something new" or "man, I need to stop scrolling on Youtube". So instead of wasting my time in things that I get nothing from, I will be wasting it here, worst case scenario I will have a site at least. (ᵕ—ᴗ—)

For the second question, "Isn't there better resources out there?", Yes, there is probably better places for you to find the same information that I will presenting here, I would recommend using other sources instead of anything in here, but I will still post things here, I will try to make here a good place to start or find something new.

Third, "am I a expert in anything?", No, I am not a expert in anything (╥ ᴗ ╥), I am you most average person on the internet, I would even say that I less than that. Still, I will try to at least  have something remotely interesting for you to read in here.

Lastly, I want to thank you for being here and I hope you find something new. (˶ᵔᗜᵔ˶)ﾉﾞ
